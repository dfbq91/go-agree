# Technical Research & Architectural Decisions: Document Generation

**Branch**: `007-document-generation` | **Date**: 2026-09-16 | **Spec**: [spec.md](./spec.md)

---

## 1. Document Generation Engines for Word (.docx) and PDF (.pdf)

### Decision
Adopt pure JavaScript/TypeScript document generation libraries that execute within standard Node.js runtime environments without native binaries or browser dependencies:
- **Word (.docx)**: Use `docx` npm package. Generates compliant OpenXML `.docx` files with structured paragraphs, headings, tables, borders, and signature lines.
- **PDF (.pdf)**: Use `pdfkit` (with `@types/pdfkit`). Generates standard PDF documents with precise layout control, typography, page numbering, and vector line drawing for signature blocks.
- Wrap both generation engines behind an application port: `DocumentGeneratorPort`.

### Rationale
- **Constitution Principle VI (Minimal Dependencies & Tooling Rigor)**: Both `docx` and `pdfkit` are self-contained pure JS libraries. They do not require headless Chromium (like Puppeteer or Playwright), LibreOffice, wkhtmltopdf, or C++ native addons (e.g., node-canvas).
- **Execution Performance**: In-memory document generation with `docx` and `pdfkit` executes in <500ms, easily satisfying the sub-5 second end-to-end target defined in SC-002.
- **Deployment Compatibility**: Serverless and containerized Node.js environments (Next.js route handlers) can run these libraries without installing Linux system packages.

### Alternatives Considered
- *Puppeteer / Chromium HTML-to-PDF*: Rejected because Chromium adds 150MB+ to bundle sizes, consumes heavy memory (200MB+ per process), introduces cold-start latency, and cannot generate editable `.docx` files natively.
- *LaTeX / Pandoc*: Rejected because it requires external CLI binaries not suitable for serverless Next.js deployments.
- *Template replacement (e.g., docxtemplater)*: Rejected because legal contracts have dynamic variable clause counts, varying paragraph lengths, and dynamic LLM clauses that require programmatic AST composition rather than brittle tag replacement.

## 2. Contract Content Assembly & Spanish Commercial Clause Anatomy

### Decision
Implement a pure domain service `ContractAssemblyService` that synthesizes standard baseline answers (Q0–Q11) and dynamic analysis answers into a traditional Spanish legal contract anatomy (clarified in Clarification 3, FR-004):
1. **Título**: Prominent uppercase heading based on the contract title and modality (e.g., "CONTRATO DE PRESTACIÓN DE SERVICIOS" or "CONTRATO DE ADQUISICIÓN DE BIENES").
2. **Comparecientes (Partes)**: Identification clause formalizing the legal capacity of both parties (individual vs legal entity, corporate status, address from Q1 and Q3).
3. **Declaraciones / Antecedentes**: Contextual background based on the core description (Q0) and delivery requirements (Q2).
4. **Cláusulas Operativas**:
   - *Cláusula Primera (Objeto)*: Clear description of services/goods (Q0).
   - *Cláusula Segunda (Condiciones de Entrega y Lugar)*: Address, delivery conditions, and milestones (Q2, Q3).
   - *Cláusula Tercera (Plazo y Vigencia)*: One-time vs recurring, duration, notice periods, and renewal rules (Q4, Q8, Q9).
   - *Cláusula Cuarta (Precio y Mecanismo de Reajuste)*: Payment terms and price adjustment mechanism for contracts >12 months (Q7).
   - *Cláusula Quinta (Obligaciones y Recursos)*: Personnel and vehicle allocation, operational duties, and legal responsibilities (Q5).
   - *Cláusula Sexta (Incumplimiento y Penalidades)*: Liability for breach and damages (Q6).
   - *Cláusula Séptima (Causales de Terminación Anticipada)*: Statutory and additional negotiated grounds for termination (Q10).
   - *Cláusula Octava (Solución de Controversias)*: Chosen dispute resolution mechanism (ordinary courts, arbitration tribunal, or conciliation) (Q11).
5. **Cláusulas Particulares (Cláusulas Dinámicas)**: Numbered clauses for each answered dynamic follow-up question generated by LLM analysis.
6. **Firmas**: Formatted signature blocks ready for external physical or digital signing by both parties, with lines for signature, printed name, legal representative, and ID number.

### Rationale
Encodes Clarifications 3 and 4 directly. Enforces strict completeness: contracts are generated only when 100% of questions are answered. Structuring the document into traditional civil/commercial sections ensures maximum legal clarity for Colombian and Spanish-speaking jurisdictions.

### Alternatives Considered
- *Unstructured Question-and-Answer list*: Rejected because users expect an authentic, formal legal agreement, not an intake transcript.
- *Fixed hardcoded text templates*: Rejected because dynamic follow-up questions from AI analysis require modular, extensible clause compilation.

## 3. Mandatory Legal Advice Disclaimer in UI & Clean Contract Body

### Decision
In accordance with Clarification 2 (FR-003):
- The explicit legal advice disclaimer MUST appear **strictly outside the document**, rendered within the application UI prior to document generation:
  - Displayed on the `SummaryReview.tsx` screen above the confirmation button.
  - Localized Spanish text (`locales/es.ts`):
    > *"Aviso importante: El presente contrato se genera automáticamente a partir de las respuestas suministradas y tiene fines exclusivamente informativos y de referencia. No constituye asesoría legal profesional. Go-Agree recomienda la revisión del documento por un abogado calificado antes de su suscripción."*
- The generated Word and PDF documents themselves MUST NOT contain platform disclaimer text or warning banners inside the contract body or footer, keeping them clean, formal, and ready for external signing.

### Rationale
Signers outside the platform (clients, vendors, counterparties) should not receive agreements cluttered with software disclaimer disclaimers, while the platform user is fully informed and legally notified prior to generating the contract.

### Alternatives Considered
- *Watermarking every PDF page with "Borrador sin valor legal"*: Rejected per user decision in Clarification 2; users need an execution-ready agreement.
- *Introductory disclaimer box inside clause 1*: Rejected per user decision in Clarification 2.

## 4. Document Storage, Single-Version Overwrite & Download Streaming

### Decision
Store generated document binary artifacts via `DocumentStoragePort`:
- The existing Supabase table `public.contract_documents` (migration `0004_contract_documents.sql`) has a unique constraint `UNIQUE (contract_id, file_format)`.
- Upon initial generation or regeneration:
  1. Compiles `.docx` buffer and `.pdf` buffer.
  2. Saves binary files to storage (Supabase Storage bucket `contracts`, or fallback storage adapter).
  3. Upserts records into `public.contract_documents` with `contract_id`, `user_id`, `file_format`, `storage_path`, and `created_at = NOW()`.
- Upon regeneration:
  - Overwrites previous storage files at the designated path.
  - Updates `created_at` timestamp on `contract_documents`.
  - Maintains only the single latest version per contract generation instance (FR-010).
- `GET /api/contracts/[id]/download?format=pdf|docx`:
  - Verifies user session and contract ownership.
  - Validates contract is in `completed` status and has compiled documents.
  - Streams file with correct MIME type and `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="contrato-[id].[format]"`.

### Rationale
Reuses existing database schema and RLS policies created in feature 006. Enforces single-version retention natively through database uniqueness constraints and storage overwrite.

### Alternatives Considered
- *Historical version table (`contract_document_versions`)*: Rejected because spec explicitly states historical versioning is out of scope.
- *On-the-fly generation on every download click without persistence*: Rejected because generating heavy documents synchronously on every click causes latency, CPU spikes, and prevents tracking document existence for dashboard gating.

## 5. Answer Modification, Regeneration State & Dashboard Gating

### Decision
In accordance with Clarifications 1 and 5:
- **Pending Regeneration Detection**:
  - A contract has pending updates (`isRegenerationPending: true`) if `contract.status === 'completed'` AND `contract.updatedAt > document.createdAt`.
- **Summary Review UI Behavior**:
  - When a user modifies an answer on a completed contract, `updatedAt` is bumped.
  - Download buttons are temporarily disabled.
  - An accessible "Actualización pendiente" banner is displayed with a primary action: "Regenerar documento".
  - Clicking "Regenerar documento" triggers `POST /api/contracts/[id]/regenerate`, re-compiles both formats, updates `document.createdAt`, and re-enables download buttons.
- **Dashboard UI Behavior**:
  - In `ContractTableRow.tsx` / `DownloadDropdown.tsx`:
    - If `isRegenerationPending === true`: Renders an accessible status badge `"Actualización pendiente"` that links directly to `/questionnaire?id={id}&mode=summary` so the user can review edits and regenerate.
    - If `hasGeneratedDocument === true && !isRegenerationPending`: Renders active format dropdown (PDF and Word).
    - If `status === 'in_progress'`: Renders disabled download button with guidance tooltip.

### Rationale
Prevents users from distributing obsolete contract files after changing business terms, while providing a clear and direct navigation path to regenerate.

## 6. Free Plan Quota Enforcement & Pro Plan Gating

### Decision
Enforce contract creation quotas using existing domain services and configuration:
- `FreeQuotaConfig.getFreeContractLimit()` reads `NEXT_PUBLIC_FREE_CONTRACTS_LIMIT` or `FREE_CONTRACTS_LIMIT` with fallback default to `3`.
- `ConsumeContractQuotaUseCase`:
  - When completing an **uncompleted** contract for the first time: Checks user tier (Free vs Pro). If Free and consumed count >= limit, throws `FreeQuotaExceededError` (HTTP 403). If under limit, increments consumed quota and proceeds.
  - When regenerating an **already completed** contract: Excluded from quota deduction (idempotent; 0 quota consumed).
- When HTTP 403 `FREE_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` is returned:
  - The UI presents `QuotaUpgradeModal` with localized Spanish explanation and a primary CTA linking to `/checkout`.

### Rationale
Ensures monetization integrity while honoring the explicit rule that editing and regenerating existing contracts never penalizes Free users.

## 7. LLM Contract Drafting Architecture, Prompt Strategy & Structured Output

### Decision
Implement an autonomous LLM drafting pipeline to transform user questionnaire answers into formal legal clauses:

1. **Input Retrieval & Transcript Assembly**:
   - `ContractRepositoryPort.getByIdAndUserId(contractId, userId)` retrieves contract title and `answers`.
   - `QuestionnaireDefinition.createStandard()` provides standard baseline question definitions.
   - `DynamicQuestionRepositoryPort.getQuestionsByContractId(contractId, userId)` retrieves all dynamic questions saved for this contract.
   - A domain formatter builds a complete, human-readable transcript combining:
     - Standard questions (Q0 to Q11) with their prompts and user answers.
     - Dynamic follow-up questions with their prompts and user answers.

2. **LLM Port & Adapter Interface**:
   - `LlmContractDraftingPort` in `packages/application`:
     ```typescript
     export interface LlmContractDraftingPort {
       draftContract(input: {
         contractId: string;
         title: string;
         transcript: string;
         answers: Record<string, unknown>;
       }): Promise<AssembledContractDTO>;
     }
     ```
   - `AiContractDraftingAdapter` in `packages/infrastructure`:
     - Reuses the existing `@ai-sdk/google` provider and Vercel AI SDK (`generateText`) pattern established in `AiQuestionAnalysisAdapter`.
     - Model: `process.env.AI_MODEL || 'gemini-3.5-flash-lite'` with fallback support for unit test mocks (`MockContractDraftingAdapter`).

3. **Prompt Engineering Strategy**:
   - **System Prompt**:
     ```text
     Eres un abogado experto en redacción de contratos comerciales y civiles en Colombia.
     Tu objetivo es redactar un contrato formal, completo y jurídicamente sólido con base en las respuestas suministradas por el usuario en el cuestionario.

     ### Estructura Contractual Obligatoria:
     1. Título formal en mayúsculas sostenidas acorde a la naturaleza del negocio (ej. CONTRATO DE PRESTACIÓN DE SERVICIOS).
     2. Comparecientes / Identificación de Partes: Contratante y Contratista (personas naturales o jurídicas, domicilios, representantes).
     3. Declaraciones y Antecedentes: Objeto del acuerdo y contexto comercial.
     4. Cláusulas Operativas Numeradas:
        - Cláusula Primera: Objeto detallado.
        - Cláusula Segunda: Lugar y condiciones de entrega/ejecución.
        - Cláusula Tercera: Plazo de ejecución, vigencia y preaviso de terminación.
        - Cláusula Cuarta: Precio, forma de pago y mecanismo de reajuste (si aplica).
        - Cláusula Quinta: Obligaciones del Contratista y asignación de personal/vehículos.
        - Cláusula Sexta: Obligaciones del Contratante.
        - Cláusula Séptima: Régimen de incumplimiento y cláusula penal.
        - Cláusula Octava: Causales de terminación anticipada.
        - Cláusula Novena: Mecanismo de solución de controversias.
     5. Cláusulas Particulares: Cláusulas adicionales derivadas de las preguntas de profundización respondidas.
     6. Bloques de Firma: Espacios formales con líneas de firma, nombre, documento de identidad y fecha para ambas partes.

     ### Reglas Estrictas:
     - NO incluyas descargos de responsabilidad ("disclaimers") dentro del cuerpo del contrato. Los avisos legales de la plataforma se muestran exclusivamente en la interfaz de usuario.
     - Redacta en español formal y jurídico colombiano.
     - No inventes obligaciones que contradigan las respuestas suministradas.
     ```
   - **User Message**:
     Sends the title and formatted transcript containing all answered standard and dynamic questions.

4. **Structured Output Enforcement (Zod Schema)**:
   - Uses `Output.object({ schema: contractDraftSchema })` guaranteeing that the LLM returns typed JSON matching `AssembledContractDTO`.
   - Each clause contains `number`, `title`, and `text`.
   - The structured JSON is fed directly into `DocumentGeneratorPort` to generate byte-identical `.docx` and `.pdf` files.

### Rationale
Ensures the contract is not a simplistic string concatenation of question answers, but an authentic, professionally drafted legal instrument reflecting the exact answers provided by the user. Structured JSON output completely decouples LLM generation from file formatting (`docx`/`pdfkit`).
