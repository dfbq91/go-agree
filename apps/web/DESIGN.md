## Brand & Style

This design system delivers institutional authority, evidentiary certainty, and modern corporate sobriety for enterprise legal workflows, contract drafting, and digital execution. Designed specifically for legal practitioners, risk officers, and enterprise corporate counsel, the aesthetic strips away superficial decoration in favor of surgical clarity, high data density, and uncompromising legibility.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Minimalist
The system implements a refined modern corporate architecture anchored in:
- **Pristine Canvas Foundations:** Paper-white surfaces set against soft neutral slate backgrounds, preventing optic fatigue during prolonged clause analysis.
- **Architectural Line Work:** Hairline borders and subtle surface level shifts rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a precise, structured drafting experience.
- **Evidentiary Contrast:** Strict, calibrated hierarchy where interactive elements invoke confidence (cobalt blue) and regulatory assurance (emerald verification seals).
- **Sobriety and Predictability:** Purposeful micro-interactions with immediate autosave feedback, deliberate confirmation flows, and persistent audit indicators.

## Colors

The color system establishes absolute legal confidence and cognitive ease. Primary actions utilize deliberate cobalt blue, signaling enterprise credibility and prompt execution. Secondary neutrals anchor descriptive text, clause subheadings, and structural lines.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Roles
- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Anchor for core interactive elements, execution buttons, active step indicators, and focused form outlines.
- **Secondary (`#4B5563`)**: Neutral anchor for metadata descriptions, secondary toolbars, and inactive toggle states.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: The seal of legal integrity and cryptographic assurance, applied strictly to signed verification states, valid checksums, and autosave indicators.
- **Neutral (`#111827`)**: Deep obsidian tone for high-contrast contract text, tabular legal data, and core headings.

### Contract Status System
Each contract lifecycle phase is bound to a low-saturation tinted background paired with high-contrast, accessible typography:
- **Borrador (Draft):** `status-draft-bg` (`#FEF3C7`) with `status-draft-text` (`#92400E`) and `status-draft-border` (`#FCD34D`).
- **En Revisión (In Review):** `status-review-bg` (`#EFF6FF`) with `status-review-text` (`#1E40AF`) and `status-review-border` (`#BFDBFE`).
- **Firmado / Completado (Signed):** `status-completed-bg` (`#ECFDF5`) with `status-completed-text` (`#065F46`) and `status-completed-border` (`#A7F3D0`).
- **Vencido / Alerta (Expired / Revoked):** `status-alert-bg` (`#FEF2F2`) with `status-alert-text` (`#991B1B`) and `status-alert-border` (`#FECACA`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on **Inter**, a neutral, high-legibility grotesque designed for screen rendering. It provides clear differentiation between numerical legal values, clause identifiers, and dense paragraph layouts.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Drafting & Clauses:** Paragraph text in contract previews must never exceed 75 characters per line (`max-w-3xl`) to optimize comprehension and legal verification.
- **Auditing & Tables:** Column data and table cell content utilize `body-sm` (`14px`) and `label-md` (`14px / 500`), while table headers enforce `badge-caps` (`11px`, bold, uppercase, tracked `0.05em`) to ensure distinct boundaries between structural labels and dynamic content.
- **Numbers & Dates:** Dates and contract hashes utilize proportional tabular lining figures to ensure vertical alignment in audit logs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an absolute 4px/8px mathematical base unit to ensure alignment across data grids and questionnaire wizards.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin` of `1rem` (16px) and `gutter` of `1rem`. Audit tables collapse into modular stacked cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md` of `1.5rem` (24px). Split clause-and-preview views transition to tabbed navigation.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with max-width boundaries:
  - Clause drafting cards & wizard questionnaires: `max-w-3xl` (768px) centered to isolate focus.
  - Contract summary and execution review: `max-w-5xl` (1024px).
  - Main operational dashboard & audit table: `max-w-7xl` (1280px).

### Spacing Density Principles
Internal card padding maintains `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (32px), creating calm space around legal questions. Micro form gaps enforce `space-sm` (8px) between labels and controls, and `space-md` (16px) between sequential questionnaire choices.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical elevations, deep blurs, or heavy ambient drops. Depth is communicated primarily through **tonal layers and crisp hairline outlines**, maintaining the crispness of formal legal documents.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Base level):** Background `#F9FAFB` provides a non-reflective ground.
2. **Sheet / Card Level (Flat 0):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a 1px border (`#E5E7EB`). Used for questionnaire steps, clause blocks, and audit tables.
3. **Elevated Elements (Elevation 1):** Floating controls, contextual toolbars, and summary cards use `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)` combined with the 1px `#E5E7EB` border.
4. **Interactive Overlays (Elevation 2):** Dropdown menus, export drawers, and autosave toasts use `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)` over a clean border.
5. **Execution Modals (Elevation 3):** Signature pads and deletion confirmation dialogues use `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)` with a backdrop tinted to `#0F172A` at 40% opacity with a `backdrop-blur-xs` (2px) layer.

## Shapes

The system uses restrained, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1` / Soft). This keeps interface elements crisp and disciplined:
- Standard inputs, buttons, and badges use `0.375rem` (6px) or `0.5rem` (8px).
- Main questionnaire cards, summary containers, and audit wrappers use `0.75rem` (12px).
- Status badges and metadata tags enforce `9999px` (fully rounded pill) to clearly separate discrete status indicators from interactive structural rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Surface `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF` (font-weight 600), radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Hover `#1D4ED8`, active `#1E40AF`. Focus outline: 2px offset with `#2563EB`.
- **Secondary Action:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#D1D5DB`, text `#374151`. Hover background `#F9FAFB` with border `#9CA3AF`.
- **Destructive Action:** Surface `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover `#B91C1C`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, text `#2563EB`, hover background `#EFF6FF`.

### Form Fields & Clause Textareas
- **Text & Area Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#D1D5DB` border, `0.5rem` border radius, placeholder `#9CA3AF`. On focus: 1px border `#2563EB` and a 2px outer ring with `#BFDBFE`.
- **Clause Radio Cards:** Stacked option tiles. Resting: 1px `#E5E7EB` on `#FFFFFF`, padding `1rem`. Active/Selected: 1px `#2563EB` on `#EFF6FF` tint with a 1px inner ring `#2563EB`.

### Status Badges (Pills)
Pill format (`rounded-full`, padding `0.125rem 0.625rem`, typography `badge-caps`):
- **Borrador:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border 1px `#FCD34D`.
- **En Revisión:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, border 1px `#BFDBFE`.
- **Firmado:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px `#A7F3D0`. Includes a 6px solid `#10B981` leading dot indicator.
- **Vencido / Alerta:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px `#FECACA`.

### Legal Audit Table
- **Header:** Background `#F9FAFB`, bottom border 1px `#E5E7EB`, text `badge-caps` in `#4B5563`.
- **Rows:** Minimum height 52px, alternating or white surface, border bottom 1px `#F3F4F6`. Hover state: `#F9FAFB`.
- **Inline Editable Title:** Text displays with an edit icon; upon click, transitions seamlessly into an active input field with bottom accent `#2563EB`.

### Legal Guidance Card (Clause Intelligence)
A contextual advisory container for ambiguous legal choices:
- Background: subtle vertical gradient from `#EFF6FF` to `#FFFFFF`.
- Border: 1px `#DBEAFE`.
- Left icon: Information badge in `#2563EB`.
- Content: Micro-tips grid with clickable preset clause phrasing options.

### Autosave & Integrity Feedback
- Positioned in the header navigation or sticky action bar.
- States:
  - *Guardando...* — Pulsing `#2563EB` indicator dot with `caption-xs` text in `#4B5563`.
  - *Guardado* — Static `#10B981` checkmark icon with text "Guardado en servidor seguro".
  - *Desconectado* — `#DC2626` warning indicator with manual retry prompt.
